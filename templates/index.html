What is being measured?

Receiver window. Free receiver capacity becomes the sender's transmission allowance. The adaptive policy tightens this allowance as utilization rises.

Pressure response. The timeline exposes every simulation tick, making it easy to explain why the window shrinks or recovers.

Scope. This is an educational transport-layer simulation—not a replacement for TCP and not a real packet sniffer.
